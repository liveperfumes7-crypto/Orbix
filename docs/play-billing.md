# Google Play build
`com.orbixcloud.app`, built with `PLAY_STORE=true`: no UPI, no WebView, no prices anywhere. Play Integrity (`play-verify`, needs `GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`)
grants 15 GB free cloud. Google Play billing / User Choice Billing code path is prepared but OFF until `play_settings` flags are enabled by the owner after Google approval.
Use a separate Play listing and branding for Orbix.
